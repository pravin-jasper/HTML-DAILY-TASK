🍝 Pasta

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta in water with some salt.
  2. Heat oil in a pan.
  3. Add onion and garlic and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  5. Add the cooked pasta.
  6. Mix well and serve hot.

🥞 Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Add milk and egg.
  3. Mix until the batter is smooth.
  4. Heat a pan and add butter.
  5. Pour the batter onto the pan.
  6. Cook both sides until golden brown.
  7. Serve with your favorite topping.

🍚 Vegetable Fried Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add onion and vegetables.
  3. Cook the vegetables for a few minutes.
  4. Add the cooked rice.
  5. Add soy sauce and salt.
  6. Mix everything well.
  7. Serve hot.
